01 / Lead response · Local MVP

LeadFlow

A new lead shouldn't get lost
between “hello” and “booked.”

A lead-management tool for local service businesses. Capture quote requests, qualify the lead, draft a reply, and keep the next follow-up in view.

Python / FlaskSQLiteLocal-first MVP
Try it right here ↓View source ↗
What works today?

The local MVP includes a public quote-request form, rule-based scoring, response drafts, pipeline statuses, an activity log, and a follow-up queue. Automatic SMS/email sending and booking integrations are future additions, not included capabilities.

02 / Client discovery · In development

SCOUTER / Glitch

Find the problem.
Make the fix worth paying for.

My Sandbox venture started with a simple workflow: audit a business's online presence, pitch a specific improvement, then do the work. SCOUTER brings discovery and client work into one place.

Node.jsBusiness discoveryClient workflow
Setup + app overview ↗Explore SCOUTER ↗
How does it fit with LeadFlow?

SCOUTER helps identify and document a business problem. LeadFlow is a possible solution when that problem is missed inquiries or weak follow-up. They are separate tools today; this is a shared workflow, not an automatic integration.

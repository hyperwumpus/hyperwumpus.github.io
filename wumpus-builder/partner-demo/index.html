Hosting and ongoing support are additional. Any lift must be feasible relative to baseline conversion. This does not establish causation.

03 / START WITH EVIDENCE

One program.
One small pilot.

  1. DISCOVER · 1 WEEKFind the actual gap.

    Review existing tools with the Bootcamp owner. Interview 5–8 participants. Agree on content rights, a sponsor, a baseline, and the success criteria.

  2. PREPARE · 2–3 WEEKSMake Builder ready to test.

    Review every prescription with the program team. Test real phones, backups, and accessibility. Add approved measurement with participant consent.

  3. LEARN · 4 WEEKSTest with 20–30 opt-in members.

    Measure activation, weekly return, logged sessions, support effort, and interview feedback. Treat purchase data as exploratory at this cohort size.

Expand only after evidence: accounts and cloud sync, approved program updates, then partner reporting and authorized integrations. Each gets its own scope.

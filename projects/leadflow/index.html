LeadFlow

A lead-management prototype for local service businesses: capture a request, qualify urgency, draft a reply and track the next follow-up.

This is a browser sample. It uses rules and a response template. Nothing is stored or sent. The separate Flask + SQLite app adds a saved dashboard, activity history and due follow-up queue.

THE ORIGIN

Why I made it

Following Bootcamp while trying to document the journey became a lot to juggle. I started with the BAMF Builder plan I was personally following, rather than converting every program. I wanted followers and participants to have a free way to track their work.

THE POSSIBILITY

The commercial idea

Provide a useful experience, create content around the journey, and potentially earn affiliate sales through approved ambassador links. Those were my intentions; the app has not demonstrated sales lift or become an official Bucked Up product.

BUILD STATUS

What to expect.

Already in the app

Builder workouts, food and progress interfaces, with browser-local tracking tools. This is the original personal build, with its own functionality and limitations.

What is limited

Only my Builder plan is represented. It has no demonstrated account/cloud system, store integration or reliable affiliate purchase attribution. Content and broad device/persistence reliability need review before a store-supported release.

What Rexburg could explore

A useful local resource that gives participants a clearer next step and a reason to stay connected to the shop. Start by asking what customers need and what the store can actually approve. A local adaptation has not been commissioned or built.

What I would measure first

Can people find and follow their next workout? Do they return? Does the store see a useful next action? Visits, completed workouts, store visits and attributed sales are different outcomes.

HyperWumpus Guitar Rig

A real-time audio experiment connecting guitar input, gain, drive, bypass, mute and stereo monitoring through a Scarlett interface.

Early concept artwork, not a screenshot of compiled software.

The current GUI targets Windows ASIO. It is not a runnable Mac app, and hardware/latency validation is pending. It has no cabinet IR, full amp model, recording or OBS send.
